# About the Data Library

The UK Parliament Data Library provides access to research publications and deposited papers from the UK Parliament.

## What's Available

- **Research Briefings** - In-depth analysis and background information on policy issues, produced by the House of Commons Library and House of Lords Library.
- **Deposited Papers** - Documents deposited in the Libraries of both Houses by government departments and other bodies.

## Data Source

All data is sourced from the UK Parliament's open data platform.
