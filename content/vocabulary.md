## Vocabulary

The UK Parliament controlled vocabulary is maintained by the House of Commons Library. It is used to index and describe the material within the Parliamentary Search application.

Our vocabulary is defined using the Simple Knowledge Organization System (SKOS) data model. SKOS is a [W3C](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Wide_Web_Consortium) standard establishing a common data model for thesauri, taxonomies, classification schemes and subject heading systems. It enables data and technology sharing across diverse applications.

The first version of the thesaurus was compiled in 1979, coinciding with the development of the Library’s first indexing application, POLIS. It’s used by indexers and cataloguers in the House of Commons Library, and indexers in the House of Lords Library. Today, the thesaurus contains:

- 13,000 concepts
- 11,000 legislation terms
- 54,000 organisations
- 12,000 names
- 49,000 synonyms
- 172 content types

The Vocabulary is available to explore at: https://api.parliament.uk/vocabulary/browser or through Parliament's [Linked Data Application](https://explore.data.parliament.uk/?learnmore=Thesaurus).
